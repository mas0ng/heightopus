How high above sea level is La Paz, Bolivia's seat of government?

3,640 m11,942 ft

Answer: 3,640 metres, or about 11,942 ft. That's about 11 times the height of the Eiffel Tower.

Did you know? Its cable cars are the highest urban cable car network in the world.
