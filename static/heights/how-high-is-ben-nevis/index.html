How high is Ben Nevis, the highest mountain in the UK?

1,345 m4,413 ft

Answer: 1,345 metres, or about 4,413 ft. That's about 4.1 times the height of the Eiffel Tower.

Did you know? Its summit is in cloud for most of the year.
