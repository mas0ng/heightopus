How high is Mont Blanc, the highest mountain in the Alps?

4,806 m15,768 ft

Answer: 4,806 metres, or about 15,768 ft. That's about 15 times the height of the Eiffel Tower.

Did you know? Its height changes slightly every year with the depth of its ice cap.
