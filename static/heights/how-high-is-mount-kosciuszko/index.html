How high is Mount Kosciuszko, mainland Australia's highest peak?

2,228 m7,310 ft

Answer: 2,228 metres, or about 7,310 ft. That's about 6.8 times the height of the Eiffel Tower.

Did you know? You can walk to the summit on a path; no climbing gear needed.
