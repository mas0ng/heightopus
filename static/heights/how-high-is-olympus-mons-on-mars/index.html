How high is Olympus Mons on Mars, the tallest volcano in the solar system?

21.9 km71,850 ft

Answer: 21.9 kilometres, or about 71,850 ft. That's about 2.5 times the height of Mount Everest.

Did you know? It is so wide that from its summit the edges would be over the horizon.
