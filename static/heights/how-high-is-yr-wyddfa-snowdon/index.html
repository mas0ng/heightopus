How high is Yr Wyddfa (Snowdon), the highest mountain in Wales?

1,085 m3,560 ft

Answer: 1,085 metres, or about 3,560 ft. That's about 3.3 times the height of the Eiffel Tower.

Did you know? A railway has carried visitors to near the summit since 1896.
