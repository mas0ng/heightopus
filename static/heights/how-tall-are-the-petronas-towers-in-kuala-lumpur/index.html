How tall are the Petronas Towers in Kuala Lumpur?

452 m1,483 ft

Answer: 452 metres, or about 1,483 ft. That's about 1.4 times the height of the Eiffel Tower.

Did you know? A skybridge links the two towers on the 41st and 42nd floors.
