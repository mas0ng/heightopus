How tall is Mauna Kea in Hawaii, measured from its base on the ocean floor?

10.2 km33,497 ft

Answer: 10.2 kilometres, or about 33,497 ft. That's about 1.2 times the height of Mount Everest.

Did you know? Measured this way, it is taller than Mount Everest.
