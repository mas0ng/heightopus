How tall is the Christ the Redeemer statue in Rio, not counting its pedestal?

30 m98.4 ft

Answer: 30 metres, or about 98.4 ft. That's about 6.8 times the height of a London double-decker bus.

Did you know? Its outstretched arms span 28 m, almost as wide as it is tall.
