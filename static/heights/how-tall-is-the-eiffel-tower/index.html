How tall is the Eiffel Tower, to the tip of its antenna?

330 m1,083 ft

Answer: 330 metres, or about 1,083 ft. That's about 3.5 times the height of the Statue of Liberty.

Did you know? The iron expands in summer heat, so the tower grows by up to 15 cm.
