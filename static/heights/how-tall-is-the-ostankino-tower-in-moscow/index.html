How tall is the Ostankino Tower in Moscow?

540 m1,772 ft

Answer: 540 metres, or about 1,772 ft. That's about 1.6 times the height of the Eiffel Tower.

Did you know? It was the world's tallest free-standing structure from 1967 to 1976.
