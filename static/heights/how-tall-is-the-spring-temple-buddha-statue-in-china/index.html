How tall is the Spring Temple Buddha statue in China, not counting its base?

128 m420 ft

Answer: 128 metres, or about 420 ft. That's about 1.4 times the height of the Statue of Liberty.

Did you know? With its lotus throne and building beneath, it reaches 208 m.
