How tall is the Statue of Liberty, from the ground to the tip of the torch?

93 m305 ft

Answer: 93 metres, or about 305 ft. That's about 21 times the height of a London double-decker bus.

Did you know? The copper statue alone is 46 m; the rest is the pedestal and foundation.
