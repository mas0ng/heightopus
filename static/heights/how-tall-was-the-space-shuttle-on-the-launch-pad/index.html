How tall was the Space Shuttle on the launch pad, with tank and boosters?

56 m184 ft

Answer: 56 metres, or about 184 ft. That's about 13 times the height of a London double-decker bus.

Did you know? The shuttle flew 135 missions between 1981 and 2011.
