How to play Heightopus

Heightopus asks how tall things are. Guess close and your little pixel octopus dives deeper into the ocean.

  1. Press Dive in for today's ten questions, or Unlimited for a random set.
  2. Each question asks how tall, or how high, something is. You have 30 seconds to type your guess.
  3. Use metres or feet with the m / ft switch, or type units like 300ft, 1.2k or 8.8km.
  4. See the real answer, how close you were and a fun fact, then watch your octopus dive.
  5. Later questions count more: ×1, ×1, ×2, ×2, ×2, ×3, ×3, ×3, ×4, ×4.

Party rooms

Press Party, choose Host a room and share the four-letter code or invite link. The host picks 5 or 10 questions and 15, 20 or 30 seconds per question. Everyone answers at once, nobody sees the other guesses until time's up, and every player's octopus dives side by side, with each person's depth shown on the gauge.

Questions and answers

Is Heightopus free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no account and nothing to install. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets, Chromebooks and computers.

When do new questions arrive?

Every day at midnight, your local time. Everyone gets the same ten questions on the same day, so you can compare results with friends.

Can I use feet instead of metres?

Yes. Use the m / ft switch next to the answer box, or type a unit with your number, like 300ft, 1.2k or 8.8km.

How do party rooms work?

Press Party, choose Host a room and share the four-letter code or invite link. Between 2 and 12 people answer the same question at the same time, and the closest guesses dive deepest.

Where do the heights come from?

Each answer uses a widely published figure. Some heights change over time or depend on how they're measured, so every question says exactly what is being measured.

Is my data stored?

Your streak, best depth and settings are saved in your own browser. When you finish a daily, only your total score is sent, anonymously, to work out how you compare with everyone else that day.
