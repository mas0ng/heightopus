Heightopus / Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated 12 November 2026.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or age.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your own device.
  • If you finish a daily, only your score is sent, with nothing that says who you are.
  • In a party room, the other players see the nickname you pick and your guesses. Don't use your real name.
  • Like every website, our host (Cloudflare) briefly handles your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them safe.

Who we are

Heightopus is made and run by Mason Gibbs (mas0ng.com), who is the data controller for the information described here. Questions or requests: mason@mas0ng.com.

What's stored on your device

Heightopus saves a few small items in your browser's local storage so it works the way you expect. They never leave your device unless stated above, and we don't set cookies. Because they're needed for the features you use, no consent banner is required under the UK's PECR rules. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them all.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, runs the party rooms and stores the anonymous daily totals, acting on our behalf. Cloudflare may process data outside the UK; it does so under approved legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Other players in a party room see your nickname, colour and guesses.
  • Sharing. If you press Post on X or WhatsApp, your result text is passed to that service, and its own privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.

Our fonts and images are served from heightopus.com itself, so loading a page doesn't contact Google or other third parties.

Young people

Heightopus is suitable for all ages and is designed to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, chats, ads or tracking, and nothing is shown publicly. Party nicknames are only seen by people in the same room, so pick a fun one rather than your real name.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information about you, object to how it's used, or ask us to limit its use. Because we don't hold accounts or link anything to you, there's usually nothing we can find. Your on-device data is in your control, and party room data is deleted automatically. If you have a request or concern, email mason@mas0ng.com.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top. Security issues can be reported through our security policy.
